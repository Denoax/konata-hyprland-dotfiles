# Showcase art direction

The public presentation borrows principles—not assets or layouts—from Mani's
portfolio constitution: true-dark dominance, restrained ion blue, asymmetric
editorial composition, precise technical typography, meaningful negative
space, and motion that preserves strong still frames.

For this project, that language is translated into Konata's quiet late-night
gaming atmosphere and the real geometry of a three-monitor Arch/Hyprland
desktop. Screenshots must come from the actual compositor and must not contain
private applications, browser history, messages, tokens, or personal files.

Generated hero artwork uses the bundled wallpaper only as a character and
palette reference. UI shown elsewhere must remain authentic screenshots.
